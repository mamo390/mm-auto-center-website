# M&M Auto Center - Product Definition

## Brand

**Name:** M&M Auto Center Inc.
**Location:** 1765 Shawson Dr Unit 14, Mississauga, Ontario, Canada
**Phone:** (647) 448-0876
**Website:** https://mamo390.github.io/mm-auto-center-website

## Business Type

- Mechanic shop & collision center
- Aftermarket parts sales (batteries, tires, exhaust)
- EV licensed vehicles
- Used car dealer (North America)
- Mobile services: glass installation, machine repair, battery replacement, fast breakdown repair

## Target Users

**Primary:**
- Vehicle owners in Mississauga and GTA
- People needing emergency auto repairs
- Fleet managers and commercial vehicle operators
- Insurance claim customers

**Secondary:**
- DIY enthusiasts looking for parts
- Used car buyers

## Brand Voice

- Professional but approachable
- Direct and efficient (no fluff)
- Trustworthy and experienced
- Mobile-first convenience

## Anti-References

- Avoid: Corporate/sterile language
- Avoid: Overly casual/slang
- Avoid: Generic auto shop clichés (wrenches everywhere)

## Strategic Principles

1. **Convenience First:** Mobile service is our differentiator
2. **Trust Through Transparency:** Clear pricing, warranty, history
3. **Expertise:** 25+ years experience should be prominent
4. **Local Focus:** Mississauga/GTA emphasis
